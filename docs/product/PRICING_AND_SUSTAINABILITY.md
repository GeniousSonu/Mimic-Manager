# Pricing & Open-Source Sustainability Model

**Status:** ACTIVE  
**Last-reviewed:** 2026-10-29  
**Applies-to-version:** v0.1.0-alpha  

---

## 1. Zero Paid Tiers & Absolute Free Sovereignty

OptiForge is and will always remain **100% Free and Open Source Software (FOSS)**.
- **No Paid Subscriptions:** There is no "Pro" tier, "Enterprise" license, or feature gating.
- **No Credit Limits:** Users can optimize 10 images or 1,000,000 images with zero marginal software cost.
- **No License Keys:** No server phoning-home to check activation status.
- **Permissive Open Source:** Core backend and PWA licensed under AGPL-3.0-only; WordPress connector licensed under GPL-2.0-or-later.

---

## 2. Long-Term Sustainability Strategy

To ensure maintenance, security patching, and multi-year longevity without commercializing the software:
1. **GitHub Sponsors & Open Collective:** Community donations from agencies and enterprises who save thousands in recurring SaaS costs.
2. **Voluntary Enterprise Support & Custom Connectors:** Independent consultants and agencies can offer commercial support, deployment hardening, and custom CMS connector development.
3. **Transparent Operational Costs:** Project infrastructure (documentation hosting, build runners) is funded via open sponsorships with transparent expense reports published on Open Collective.
